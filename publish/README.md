# AI Money Tracker

An iOS budgeting app that turns a spending plan into a straight answer: what
comes in, what goes out, and what to fix first.

---

## 📄 Privacy Policy

**[Read the full Privacy Policy →](PRIVACY.md)**

In short:

- **Your budget stays on your phone.** It is saved locally and never uploaded.
- **We never see your bank username or password.** Bank sign-in happens inside
  Plaid's own secure screen.
- **No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no data sales.**

## 🛟 Support

Questions, bug reports, account or data-deletion requests:

### Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device removes the locally stored budget immediately
— we never had a copy of it.

To delete your **account** and anything held server-side, use
**About → Delete Account** in the app — it is immediate and disconnects your
bank connections as part of the same step. If you can no longer access the
app, email the address above from the address associated with your account and
we'll run the same deletion by hand (allow up to 30 days; we'll confirm once
it's done). Details in
[the privacy policy](PRIVACY.md#5-how-long-it-is-kept-and-how-to-have-it-deleted).
