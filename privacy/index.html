Privacy Policy

Ai Money Tracker  •  Version 1.1  •  Effective Date: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ai Money Tracker ("we," "our," or "the app") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use our iOS application. We built this app to help you track and understand your finances, and we handle your data with that responsibility in mind. This policy is displayed in-app and is available at this public link at all times.

1. Information We Collect

We follow data minimization: we collect only what is needed to provide the app's functionality.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not collect data beyond what is listed here without updating this policy first.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the app's core features (linking accounts, tracking transactions, budgeting)
  • To authenticate your identity and secure your account, including multi-factor authentication
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests
  • To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • To improve app performance and reliability through crash reporting and aggregated usage analytics
  • To comply with applicable law

3. How We Share Your Information

We share information only with the following categories of third parties, and only as needed to operate the app:

  • Plaid — to securely link your bank accounts and retrieve balance and transaction data. Plaid's own privacy policy governs its handling of your data during that process.
  • Stripe — to process payments, using tokenization so we never handle your raw card details.
  • Cloud infrastructure and authentication providers — to host our backend and secure sign-in (including MFA), under contractual confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — only where required by law, subpoena, or valid legal process.

We do not share your financial data with advertisers, and we do not sell your information to data brokers.

4. Data Security

We maintain a documented information security program covering how data is protected, including encryption in transit and at rest, restricted administrative access, secrets management, regular patching, and a defined incident response process. Sign-in to the app is protected with multi-factor authentication (via Sign in with Apple or a managed authentication provider supporting TOTP/SMS), which is required before you can link a bank account.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and financial data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the app's services. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export a copy of your data
  • Withdraw consent for optional data uses, such as analytics
  • Unlink a connected bank account at any time from within the app

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Ai Money Tracker is not directed to children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We review this policy at least annually and update it whenever our data practices materially change. The "Effective Date" above reflects the most recent update, and continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
